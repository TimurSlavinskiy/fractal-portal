How to explore

Each canvas pixel starts at a complex coordinate z. Repeated application of z → z² + c reveals the Julia set. Scroll or use + and − to zoom; drag or use touch to move; press 0 to reset. Use Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z for history. Zoom is intentionally limited to 32×: conventional GPU floating-point precision is not sufficient for arbitrary deep zoom. The visual glow is an artistic interpretation, not a mathematical invariant.
